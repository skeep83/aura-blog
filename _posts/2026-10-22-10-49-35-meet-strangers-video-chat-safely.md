## Your half of the deal: six rules that actually matter

Platform features are the seatbelt; these are the driving habits. From years of documented incidents on random chat platforms, the ones that prevent real harm are unglamorous:

- **Nothing identifying in the first five minutes.** No full name, neighborhood, workplace, school uniform, or the street sign behind you. Backgrounds leak more than people do.
- **Keep it on the platform.** The first "let's move to WhatsApp/Instagram" is a fork in the road. Moving off-platform means leaving the moderation and reporting you just relied on.
- **No money, ever, to a stranger you met on video chat.** Not a loan, not a "verify your account" link, not a crypto tip. Any monetary request from a random match is a red flag, full stop.
- **Use report before you rationalize.** One weird second is survivable; the sunk-cost instinct to "give them another minute" is how bad encounters extend. One tap costs you nothing.
- **Assume the other side is recording.** Your platform may not record; a stranger's phone absolutely can. Don't do anything on camera you couldn't tolerate in a group chat.
- **Protect the account behind the chat.** Since Aura runs on your Telegram identity, your Telegram security *is* your chat security: cloud password on, active sessions checked, unknown DMs treated as spam until proven otherwise.

## FAQ

### Is it safe to meet strangers on video chat in 2026?

Materially safer than the Omegle era, because enforced 18+ gates, AI moderation, and instant reporting are now table stakes. It is not risk-free: you're still choosing to be on camera with a stranger. The risk profile of a moderated, 18+, report-friendly service like Aura or OmeTV is closer to "walking in a busy public place" than "meeting someone from a dating app at their apartment" — manageable with normal judgment.

### Does "not recorded" mean my video chat is private?

It means the platform doesn't store the call — Aura's P2P calls never touch a server as recordings. It does not mean anonymous: your Telegram account is attached to the session, and P2P connections expose your IP address to your match. The stranger's own screen recording is also outside anyone's control. "Not recorded" is one layer of privacy, not the whole thing.
