## The problem with every chatroulette alternative so far

Two products defined the category and neither aged well. Chatroulette kept its moderation reputation intact by never fixing it. Omegle tried harder and lost — it shut down in November 2023 under the weight of misuse and legal costs. Everything since has picked one of two bad options:

- **Clone the layout, skip the problem.** Endless "random video chat" sites with the same grid of strangers and the same unmoderated feed.
- **Fix the chaos, kill the fun.** Apps that gate you behind a download, an email signup, a phone-number verification, and often a subscription before you see a single face.

Meanwhile the thing people loved — instant, anonymous, zero-friction — got optimized out of existence. A real alternative has to be as fast as Chatroulette's original Next button and as moderated as a modern platform, without asking you to install anything.
