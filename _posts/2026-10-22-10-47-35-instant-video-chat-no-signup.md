## What "instant" actually requires

To be honestly instant, a video chat needs four things at once:

1. **Zero install.** If it lives in an app store, you've already lost the impulse moment. Aura opens from a bot link — if Telegram is on your phone, you're done installing.
2. **Zero registration.** Your identity is your existing Telegram account. No email, no phone re-verification, no username crisis.
3. **A two-question setup.** Aura asks your gender and your language — one of 20+ — and nothing else. Two taps, then Start.
4. **A one-tap exit.** Next skips to a new person instantly. An instant service that traps you in bad conversations isn't instant, it's a hostage situation.

## What you get once you're connected

The call itself is peer-to-peer video, and calls are **not recorded** — when it ends, nothing is stored on a server. Two features earn special mention for people who want instant but not exposed:

- **Emoji face-mask mode.** Cover your camera with an emoji and still chat. Your match can see you're masked — it's transparent, not deceptive — which makes it a polite middle ground between voice-only and full video.
- **Icebreaker mini-games.** Two quick games you play together on screen. They exist because instant matching has a real problem: the first fifteen seconds are often two people saying "hey" "hi" "so" in a loop. Something to do fixes that better than any prompt.

On the safety side: 18+ only, AI moderation scanning content, and one-tap reporting that actually removes people. No random video chat eliminates bad actors completely — Omegle learned that and paid for it in November 2023 — but the combination of age gate, AI moderation, and instant reporting is the current responsible baseline.
