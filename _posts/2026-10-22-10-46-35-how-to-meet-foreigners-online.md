The difficulty was never "finding foreigners." It is everything around the finding:

- **Apps eat the intent.** Language-exchange apps like Tandem or HelloTalk work, but they are text-first, slow, and half the users want a friend or a date, not a conversation.

## What to actually talk about

Meeting foreigners online is a skill, and it helps to go in with something to do. A few patterns that work:

- **Ask for the local version of something ordinary.** Not "what is your country like" but "what does lunch at your school/work look like?" Concrete questions get concrete answers.
- **Use the mini-games.** They exist because two strangers performing small talk is awkward. A game gives the call a shape.
- **Trade one fact each.** Teach them one word in your language, learn one in theirs. It takes ten seconds and breaks the formality.
- **Keep Next in your pocket.** Random means random. A dud call costs one tap, not an apology.
