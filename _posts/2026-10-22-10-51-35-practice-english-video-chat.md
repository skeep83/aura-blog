## A practice routine that actually builds fluency

Random calls work best with a plan. A 20-minute session that works:

- **Call 1–2: warm-up.** Small talk only — where you're from, what you do. Low stakes, gets the mouth warmed up.
- **Call 3–4: one concrete question.** "What's a normal Tuesday like for you?" Concrete questions produce longer, richer answers than "how are you."
- **Call 5: use a mini-game.** Games pull both people out of formal interview mode and produce the most natural speech.
- **After the session:** write down three words or phrases you didn't understand and looked up or asked about. That's your vocabulary list for the day, and you earned it in real conversation.

Two rules: never end a call you understood perfectly — push until one call stumps you, that's where learning happens; and keep mask mode as a training wheel, not a lifestyle. If shyness is the blocker, start masked and unmask after a few sessions.
