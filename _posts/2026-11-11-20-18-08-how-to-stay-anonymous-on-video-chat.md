# Anonymous Video Chat: What You Actually Hide and What You Show

"Anonymous video chat" is a contradiction the industry sells anyway: the camera shows your face to a stranger, so total anonymity is gone the second you press Start. What is still on the table is something more useful — practical anonymity. Your phone number, email, real name, social graph and location never have to enter the conversation. Here is what anonymous video chat can and cannot mean in 2026, and how Aura, a random video chat inside Telegram, draws the line.

## What "anonymous" can mean on a video call

There are three different things people mean when they search for anonymous video chat:

1. **Identity anonymity** — the stranger cannot look you up: no name, no phone number, no profile to Google you from.
2. **Data anonymity** — the service itself holds as little about you as possible: no email database, no contact-list upload, no ad profile built from your chats.
3. **Visual anonymity** — nobody sees your face. Avatar chats deliver this; video chats, by definition, do not.

A decent product is honest about which one it offers. Aura offers the first two fully and drops the third, because it is a video chat: your face is the point. What it never asks for is anything beyond the Telegram account you already have.

## Where the anonymity ends

Be clear-eyed about the limits:

- **Your face is the identifier.** Anyone who recognizes you recognizes you. The same rule applies in reverse: what your room shows in the background is also public.
- **Screenshots exist.** No video chat can prevent someone from capturing a frame. Treat every call like a live broadcast to a single viewer.
- **Your Telegram account is the login.** The service knows your Telegram identity; your match does not. That is the trade for skipping registration entirely — one existing credential instead of a new email-and-password pair stored on someone's server.

For most adults — Aura is 18+ — this is the right trade: the exposure is exactly the video call itself, and nothing around it gets harvested.
